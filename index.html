A HADITH REMINDER

A date-palm in Paradise

مَنْ قَالَ سُبْحَانَ اللَّهِ الْعَظِيمِ وَبِحَمْدِهِ غُرِسَتْ لَهُ نَخْلَةٌ فِي الْجَنَّةِ

Subḥānallāhil-ʿAẓīm wa biḥamdih.

“Whoever says, ‘Glory is to Allah, the Magnificent, and with His Praise,’ a date-palm tree is planted for him in Paradise.”

Jamiʿ at-Tirmidhi 3464 · Book 48, Hadith 95

Grading differs: al-Tirmidhi described it as hasan sahih gharib; the Darussalam grading displayed by Sunnah.com is daʿif. Other hadith scholars, including al-Albani, graded it authentic. Please consult a trusted scholar for guidance.
